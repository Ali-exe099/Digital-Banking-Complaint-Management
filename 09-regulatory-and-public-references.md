## Regulatory-use limitations

- The commercial-bank Internal Ombudsman Directions must not be applied automatically to every bank, payments provider or other regulated entity.
- The applicable entity-specific direction, complaint purview, exclusions and clock rules must be checked by Compliance/Legal.
- RBI FAQs are explanatory material. The underlying Scheme and applicable directions remain the controlling sources.
- This portfolio project does not provide a legal opinion or substitute for a bank compliance assessment.
