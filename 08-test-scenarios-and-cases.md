> **Execution disclaimer:** These are proposed test designs for a product concept. No application has been built or connected to a bank system, and no test has been executed. All cases below therefore have status **Not Run**. Expected results depend on bank-approved policy, configuration and interfaces. Use synthetic test data only.

## 1. Purpose

Define test conditions that can be used to validate the functional requirements and business rules in the project. This document expands the 17 high-level scenarios in the FRD and addresses the test-design gaps identified by the RTM.

## 2. Test scope

### In scope

- Complaint intake, validation, reference generation and duplicate review.
- Classification suggestions, agent confirmation and override.
- Fraud/security routing.
- Team assignment, reassignment and ownership history.
- Status lifecycle and investigation notes.
- TAT selection, calculation, ageing and operational escalation.
- Customer communication and delivery outcomes.
- Final disposition and applicable Internal Ombudsman referral/review controls.
- Audit history, access controls, reporting and configuration administration.
- Non-functional test planning for performance, availability, privacy, accessibility and integrations.

### Out of scope until a target implementation is identified

- Executing actual payment reversals, refunds or chargebacks.
- Testing a named bank's core banking or payment network integration.
- Production security penetration testing.
- Verifying live regulatory submission or RBI CMS connectivity.
- Confirming regulatory interpretation; Compliance/Legal must approve the applicable rule set.

## 3. Test approach

| Test level | Purpose | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Functional | Confirm a feature behaves as specified | Complaint registration or assignment |
| Negative / validation | Confirm invalid action or input is rejected safely | Missing mandatory field or prohibited status transition |
| Role / permission | Confirm users can only perform authorised actions | Agent attempts admin rule change |
| Integration | Confirm confirmed interfaces exchange correct information | Notification service acknowledgement, where selected |
| Data / audit | Confirm information is stored accurately and history is traceable | Before/after audit values |
| Non-functional | Confirm quality targets after they are approved | Response time, load, recovery, accessibility |
| Regulatory rule validation | Confirm configured eligibility and time rules after Compliance approval | Applicable Internal Ombudsman referral boundary |

## 4. Preconditions and test data

### 4.1 General preconditions

- A test environment and build/version are identified by the delivery team.
- The test role accounts and permissions are configured.
- Test complaint categories, routing mappings, priority criteria and TAT rules are approved for the test environment.
- Date/time zone, business calendar, holiday calendar and receipt-time rules are confirmed where used.
- Notification and external-service dependencies are either connected to test endpoints or explicitly stubbed.
- Regulatory eligibility and exceptions have been approved by Compliance for the scenarios that use them.

## 7. Entry and exit criteria

### Entry criteria

- Requirement baseline and approval status are known.
- The relevant build and test environment are available.
- Roles, configurations and test data are prepared.
- Regulatory conditions in the test are approved by Compliance.
- Test steps and expected results are reviewed by QA and the business owner.

### Exit criteria

The business owner and QA lead should define the required pass rate and defect thresholds. At minimum:

- All release-critical cases have an execution result.
- No open critical defect remains without a formally accepted disposition.
- Requirement-to-test traceability is updated.
- Regulatory and access-control cases have evidence where applicable.
- Failed, blocked and not-applicable cases have a recorded explanation.
- Test evidence is stored in an approved location with sensitive information redacted.
